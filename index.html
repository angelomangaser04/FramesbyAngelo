01 · About me

AI-Assisted Video Editor · Graphic Motion Artist

Hi, I'm Michael Angelo

I’m an AI-assisted video editor with a Graphic Motion Artist’s eye. I build AI into every stage of production: scripting and hooks with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, AI visuals and b-roll with Higgsfield, and natural voiceovers with ElevenLabs. Then I bring it all together in CapCut, Premiere Pro, and After Effects with clean cuts, dynamic whiteboard animations, and polished motion graphics. The result is scroll-stopping ads made faster without cutting corners on quality. I’m highly organized, deliver on tight deadlines, and work well with teams in fast-paced production.

My edit style
Hooks that land in 3 seconds, cuts on the beat, and motion that always has a reason.
Formats I cut
UGC ads, Reels & TikToks, podcast clips, explainers, and promo videos.
What I care about
Story first. Every frame has to earn its place on the timeline.
Delivered as
9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 cuts, on-brand and on deadline.
